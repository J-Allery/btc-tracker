How the blend is calculated

The blend is the median of quotes that arrived in the last 10 seconds. Coinbase, Kraken, and Binance.US contribute their last trade. Bitstamp contributes the midpoint of its best bid and ask, because its trades print less often. Binance global prints BTCUSDT; that price is multiplied by a USDT/USD rate, the median of fresh Coinbase, Kraken, and Binance.US prints, held for up to 60 seconds if the peg goes quiet. A feed older than 10 seconds leaves the median. The spread is the highest fresh quote minus the lowest.
